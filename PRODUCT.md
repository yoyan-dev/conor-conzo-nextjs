# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults starting or returning to gym training, and people seeking sustainable personal or online coaching. Visitors need to understand the coaching options, feel welcome regardless of experience, and find a low-pressure next step. Brands, creators, and gyms are a secondary audience for collaborations.

## Current Delivery Scope

UI-only development planning. The latest primary visual selection is Figma node 14:56. Combine the main Design visual system with Figma Make interaction ideas and the document copy. Live booking, email sending, persistence, and production deployment are deferred. See docs/UI-DEVELOPMENT-PLAN.md for the active roadmap.

## Product Purpose

A public TRYHRD / Coach Conzo landing page that explains personal training, online coaching, orientations, and collaborations. The source document prioritises strength, confidence, consistency, and fitness for life. A free 30-minute discovery call is the provisional primary conversion; the user has not yet answered the conversion-goal question.

## Brand Personality

Welcoming, human, playful. Use the supplied copy's judgment-free attitude and practical coaching voice. Retain the dark visual identity and layout with usability improvements, confirmed on 2026-10-14. The primary reference is Figma Design iP7VbSVY0JB6hOmDLLPTb5; the first Figma Make is inspiration only. The primary design includes a restrained blue accent. The supplied Google Doc governs public copy.

## Anti-references

Avoid aggressive body-shaming fitness advertising, unsupported transformation promises, invented social proof, and replacing the supplied dark design with an unrelated visual direction. These principles follow the supplied copy and the user's fidelity preference.

## Design Principles

1. Make beginners feel comfortable and capable.
2. Preserve the recognisable Figma composition while improving readability and mobile use.
3. Explain each offer before asking visitors to commit.
4. Let approved copy and genuine evidence carry trust.
5. Make every link and conversion path lead somewhere useful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Engineering baseline: WCAG 2.2 AA, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced-motion support, semantic structure, and responsive content. This is a project baseline rather than a claim of completed compliance. No additional user-specific accommodation has been supplied.
